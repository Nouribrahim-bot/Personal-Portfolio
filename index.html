[ 01 ] About
Nour Ibrahim ↪ B.Arch student

Designing with the slow,
deliberate logic of place.

I'm an architecture student working between drawing, model and code. My work investigates how everyday rituals — meeting, waiting, gathering — shape the spaces we build, and how those spaces in turn rehearse how we live.

From a 1st-place winning asylum proposal to a nursery in Senegal, an art-hub in Byblos, and affordable container housing in Beirut — each project is treated as a small argument about scale, material and memory. I work fluidly across plan, section, axonometric, render and physical model.
